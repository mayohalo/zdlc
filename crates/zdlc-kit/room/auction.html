A sealed-bid auction. The price is public, the bidders are not.

Four bidders, you and three house bidders, each lock the same deposit in a contract with the seller and send a sealed bid to the auctioneer. The highest bid wins and pays the second-highest. Two oracles rule each contract "won at" the price or "lost", and every contract closes into shielded notes. The world learns the clearing price; not who bid what, and not who won. Every transaction is real, on Zcash testnet.

      What happened

        The auctioneer sees the bids (a commit-reveal or threshold scheme would remove that trust) but never touches the funds: the deposits sit in 2-of-2s with the seller, and only the oracles' rulings choose which pre-signed payout completes. The bidders and the seller run as zDLC nodes on this server so you can play the round, so here all four deposits come from one wallet's transparent coins; real bidders would each fund theirs from their own shielded notes with a deposit link (as in the escrow room), leaving nothing to connect them. Demo funds are testnet coins. Not audited; do not use zDLC for real money yet.
